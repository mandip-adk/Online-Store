Our Story

From a Small Farm to Your Doorstep

Organic Store was founded with a simple idea — make fresh, chemical-free vegetables and fruits accessible to every household in Nepal. What started as a small weekend market stall has grown into a trusted online store serving hundreds of families.


Our mission is to support local farmers, promote sustainable agriculture, and ensure that the food you eat is as pure and nutritious as nature intended.
